## Community Sources

- `ps4.community_wiki`, `ps4.techniques_wiki`, `ps4.pscave_notes`, and `ps4.zh_wiki_title` are used for factual cross-checking only.

## Localized-Name Sources

- `ps4.localized_name_audit` records project-reviewed Chinese aliases and ASR variants.
- Ambiguous transliterations remain in planning docs until source-backed.

## Rejected Sources

- Copied fan-translation scripts.
- Full walkthrough text dumps.
- Long item, Technique, or combination-attack tables.
